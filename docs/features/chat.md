# 💬 Chat

| | |
|---|---|
| **Direct messages & group chats** | Message requests (with Block, to stop someone sending more), replies, emoji reactions, edits, deletions and unread counts |
| **Pinned messages** | Anyone in a chat can pin a message (up to 50 per chat) from the message menu; it gets a pin on its bubble and a notice in the chat. The pin in the chat's header lists them with Jump. Whoever pinned it, group admins, either person in a DM and site admins (admin panel) can unpin |
| **Class chats** | Everyone at HTX is put in their class's group chat automatically (no message request). It follows the class through the years and is renamed every July 1st (1.X → 2.X). Nobody is an admin, and it can't be left or added to; changing class moves you to the other one |
| **Group roles** | Group owners and admins can rename the group, change its icon and background, unpin anyone's pins, and add, kick, mute and promote members |
| **Message groups** | Messages sent in a row by the same person (less than 10 minutes apart) are shown together, and pointing at one shows when it was sent |
| **Typing indicator** | "Alice is typing..." shows just above the message box for everyone else in the chat while someone is typing (up to 3 names, then "Several people are typing..."). It goes away when they send, empty the box or stop for a few seconds. Comes along with the chat's normal poll, so it costs no extra requests |
| **Chat notices** | Changes in a group (new name, new admin, a new nickname, someone joined) show up in the chat |
| **Nicknames & profiles** | Give the person you're DMing a nickname only you see (chat menu), and open their profile straight from the chat. In a group, anyone can give anyone (themselves too) a nickname from Members, which everyone in the group sees them by, with a notice in the chat and a notification for whoever got it; the member list keeps the username next to it. Not while muted, and one given while ghost banned only shows to whoever gave it. Nicknames (DM and group) follow the name/bio rules: Latin alphabet, numbers, punctuation and emoji only, and no blocked links |
| **GIFs & smileys** | GIF picker powered by Giphy, plus a smiley menu. A message of only emoji (up to 75) is shown big, without a bubble |
| **Attachments** | Send files and pictures, up to 10 (50 MB together) in one message: pick them, paste them or drop them on the chat, and they wait as preview tiles above the message box. Several pictures show as a gallery, and the picture viewer goes through them with the arrows. Pictures and videos show without a bubble, name or size, like GIFs; right-click → File info shows the name, type, size, sender and expiry (a picture opens the viewer with Info open). Videos (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV) get a player in the chat. Pictures are turned into JPEG XL in the browser (lossless, or visually lossless for JPEGs), videos into WebM + VP9 with Opus sound (at most 1080p; sent as they are when "Keep original videos" is ticked or the browser can't), other files are zipped on the server. Everyone has 10 tickets of 5 MB, site admins 20 (admins can give or take away more), which come back when an attachment expires (90 days, or 3 for big ones) or is deleted |
| **Formatting** | Discord-style `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `__underline__`, `~~strikethrough~~`, `` `code` `` and code blocks, `#` headings, lists and `[masked](https://links)`, from a right-click menu on the message box or Ctrl+B / I / U |
| **@mentions** | Type `@` for a list of the people in the chat. Mentions light up, and the person mentioned gets a notification (not from muted chats or people they blocked). Works in Chess, Matador and UNO's game chats too |
| **Chat menu** | Right-click a chat to see its info (type, created when and by who, members, messages, when you joined), mute its notifications for a while, hide it or leave it |
| **Spam limit** | Everyone can send up to 10 messages every 5 seconds, in all their chats together (files and GIFs count too). One over that isn't sent, and stays in the message box with a popup saying to slow down |
| **Link safety** | Links ask before they open, and links to IP loggers and other sites on the URL blacklist (`src/assets/urlBlacklist.txt`) can't be sent at all, in chats or game chats |
