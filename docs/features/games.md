# 🎲 Games

## Matador

| | |
|---|---|
| **Matador** | The Danish Monopoly for 2-4 players. Games can be public, joined with a 6-character code, or invite-only |
| **House rules** | One dice (a 6 gives another roll), no auctions: a property nobody buys stays with the bank. Get out of jail by paying, using a "benådet" card or rolling a 6. Taxes and Prøv lykken payments go into Parkering's account, and whoever lands on Parkering gets it (a room setting, on by default) |
| **Full game** | Houses and hotels, mortgages, Prøv lykken cards, the income tax choice, and trading properties, money and cards with the other players at any time |
| **Turn timer** | 30 seconds to act, reset by every move. Run out and your turn is passed on. The timer pauses while you owe money, and the host can pause the whole game |
| **Saves** | The game saves after every move. Leave and your seat is kept for you. The host can give an empty seat to someone else, or save and stop the game to continue it later |
| **In-game chat** | A chat for each game, plus a sound when it's your turn |

## Chess

| | |
|---|---|
| **Chess** | Two-player chess with every rule: castling, en passant, promotion (only to a piece your opponent has taken from you; until then a pawn waits on the last rank, and the choice pops up the moment one is taken), and draws by stalemate, repetition, the 50-move rule and too few pieces. Public, code or invite-only games, with or without a clock (3+2 up to 30 minutes), set in the game's settings. The host starts the game once someone has joined |
| **Chess extras** | Draw offers, resigning, aborting before both have moved, claiming the win when your opponent has gone, the host pausing the game (and the clocks), rematches with swapped colours, arrows and highlighted squares drawn with the right mouse button (like chess.com), an in-game chat and a leaderboard |

## UNO

| | |
|---|---|
| **UNO** | The card game for 2-6 players, with the full 108-card deck: numbers, Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, Wild and Wild Draw Four (which can be played at any time). Public, code or invite-only games. The host starts the game once someone has joined, and a new one when it's over; new players can join between games |
| **Calling UNO** | Press UNO! when you play your second-to-last card. Forget, and anyone can catch you before the next player moves: you draw 2 |
| **Settings** | The host picks the time for a turn (15, 30 or 60 seconds), stacking (answer a Draw Two with a Draw Two, or a Wild Draw Four with a Wild Draw Four, to pass them all on) and drawing until you can play, in the game's settings |
| **Turn timer** | Run out of time and you draw a card (or the cards waiting for you), and your turn is over. The host can pause the game |
| **Leaving** | Leaving a game being played (or having it closed for 60 seconds) takes you out of it and counts as a loss. Your cards go back into the deck, and the last player left in wins |
| **Extras** | Official scoring (the winner scores the cards left in everyone else's hands), a log of what happened, an in-game chat with @mentions, a sound when it's your turn, and a leaderboard with wins, win %, points and best win |

## Wordle

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wordle** | The daily word game: guess the five-letter word in six tries. There's an English and a Danish word every day (the Danish keyboard has æ, ø and å), the same for everyone, with stats, sharing and a leaderboard for each language with win %, average guesses and the current and longest streak |

## Space Ore Miner

| | |
|---|---|
| **Space Ore Miner** | A clicker game, like Cookie Clicker: click the asteroid for ore, and spend it on 15 buildings (from a Space Pickaxe Guy to a Multiverse Drill) that mine for you. Each one costs 15% more than the last |
| **Upgrades** | Click upgrades (critical clicks, clicks worth a share of your production, an Auto-Clicker Bot and Swarm), six upgrades per building at 10, 25, 50, 100, 150 and 200 owned, synergies between buildings, and comet upgrades (one of them lets you catch comets with the C key) |
| **Golden Comets** | A comet flies across the screen now and then. Catch it for a pile of ore, 7× production or 77× clicks for a while, or (rarely) a Diamond Comet with 777× production |
| **Warp Jump** | Prestige: after a billion ore in a run, start over in a new galaxy for Dark Matter, ∛(ore mined in this universe ÷ 1 billion). Every Dark Matter earned is +5% production (7.5% with Dark Matter Reactor) until the next Big Crunch, and it buys upgrades that last until then too |
| **Big Crunch** | The prestige after Warp Jump: after earning 2,000 Dark Matter in a universe, collapse it (Dark Matter, its upgrades and all) for Antimatter, √(Dark Matter ÷ 100). Every Antimatter earned is +10% production until the next Big Rip, and Warp Jumps get the square root of that as more Dark Matter (all of it made Antimatter double faster and faster until it ran into Infinity), and it buys upgrades (keep the Dark Matter upgrades, twice the Dark Matter, start runs with ore, keep upgrades through Warp Jumps, stronger comets) |
| **Big Rip** | The last prestige: after earning 10,000 Antimatter in a multiverse, tear it apart (Antimatter, its upgrades and everything a Big Crunch takes) for Strange Matter: 21 for 10,000 Antimatter and 20 more for every ten times as much. Like a Warp Jump, a rip only gives what that's more than all the Strange Matter earned before, so each one has to beat your best multiverse, and it can't run away like Antimatter did. Every Strange Matter ever earned is +100% production for good |
| **Skill tree** | Strange Matter buys 52 skills, kept for good, in a tree that grows from the Strangelet on the left to the right, one row per branch, with links between neighbouring branches: a skill can be learned once every skill linked to the left of it is, including ones in the branch next to it. Five branches: Industry (buildings, prices, starting buildings, keeping upgrades through Warp Jumps), Hands (clicks, crits, auto-clicks), the Core across the middle (big production boosts), Sky (comets) and Cosmos (more Dark Matter, Antimatter and Strange Matter, keeping the Antimatter upgrades, more ore while away, and the Autopilot and Auto Crunch switches that Warp Jump and Big Crunch by themselves). They all meet in the Theory of Everything on the right. Pick a skill to see what it does, what it needs and what it costs in a card beside it, over the tree |
| **Views** | A menu switches between four views without loading the page again (so the game keeps running): Ore (the asteroid, which stays put, next to the store, which scrolls), Dark Matter (the Warp Jump), Antimatter (the Big Crunch, locked until the first Warp Jump) and Strange Matter (the Big Rip and the skill tree, locked until the first Big Crunch). The menu shows your Dark Matter, Antimatter and Strange Matter and a dot when there's something to do in a view. Stats, achievements and how to play are popups from the buttons by the title |
| **Extras** | 51 achievements, an asteroid that changes as you mine more, rock bits and floating numbers on every click, a news ticker, sounds and a leaderboard |
| **Hot keys** | Space mines, C catches a comet (after buying the Comet Hot Key upgrade), 1-9 and 0 buy the buildings in the order they're listed, U buys the cheapest affordable upgrade, B the affordable building with the most ore/s for its price, A switches between buying 1/10/100, W opens the Warp Jump, X the Big Crunch, R the Big Rip and M turns the sound on/off. Holding Space only mines once, holding 1-9, 0, U or B keeps buying |
| **Saving** | Saved on the server every 30 seconds and when you leave, and you keep mining at 5% while you're away. The server plays every click and purchase again to check it, so the leaderboard can't be cheated from the browser, and clicks only count up to 20 a second. Clicks at exactly the same pace for 20 seconds (an auto-clicker or a held-down key), or 40 clicks in a row whose gaps vary less than 10% (standard deviation of the gaps, as a share of the average gap), only mine 20%, until the pace changes. The game runs in one tab at a time |

The Games section can be opened to everyone, only to admins, or to nobody with `games_access` in the
[config](../config.md), and players can block it for themselves (see [Account & security](account.md)).
