# 🎲 Games

## Matador

| | |
|---|---|
| **Matador** | The Danish Monopoly for 2-4 players. Games can be public, joined with a 6-character code, or invite-only |
| **House rules** | One dice (a 6 gives another roll), no auctions: a property nobody buys stays with the bank. Get out of jail by paying, using a "benådet" card or rolling a 6. Taxes and Prøv lykken payments go into Parkering's account, and whoever lands on Parkering gets it (a room setting, on by default) |
| **Full game** | Houses and hotels, mortgages, Prøv lykken cards, the income tax choice, and trading properties, money and cards with the other players at any time |
| **Turn timer** | 30 seconds to act, reset by every move. Run out and your turn is passed on. The timer pauses while you owe money, and the host can pause the whole game |
| **Saves** | The game saves after every move. Leave and your seat is kept for you. The host can give an empty seat to someone else, or save and stop the game to continue it later |
| **In-game chat** | A chat for each game, plus a sound when it's your turn |

## Chess

| | |
|---|---|
| **Chess** | Two-player chess with every rule: castling, en passant, promotion (only to a piece your opponent has taken from you; until then a pawn waits on the last rank, and the choice pops up the moment one is taken), and draws by stalemate, repetition, the 50-move rule and too few pieces. Public, code or invite-only games, with or without a clock (3+2 up to 30 minutes), set in the game's settings. The host starts the game once someone has joined |
| **Chess extras** | Draw offers, resigning, aborting before both have moved, claiming the win when your opponent has gone, the host pausing the game (and the clocks), rematches with swapped colours, arrows and highlighted squares drawn with the right mouse button (like chess.com), an in-game chat and a leaderboard |

## Wordle

| | |
|---|---|
| **Wordle** | The daily word game: guess the five-letter word in six tries. There's an English and a Danish word every day (the Danish keyboard has æ, ø and å), the same for everyone, with stats, sharing and a leaderboard for each language with win %, average guesses and the current and longest streak |

## Space Ore Miner

| | |
|---|---|
| **Space Ore Miner** | A clicker game, like Cookie Clicker: click the asteroid for ore, and spend it on 15 buildings (from a Space Pickaxe Guy to a Multiverse Drill) that mine for you. Each one costs 15% more than the last |
| **Upgrades** | Click upgrades (critical clicks, clicks worth a share of your production, an Auto-Clicker Bot and Swarm), three upgrades per building at 10, 25 and 50 owned, upgrades for every building, synergies between buildings, and comet upgrades (one of them lets you catch comets with the C key) |
| **Golden Comets** | A comet flies across the screen now and then. Catch it for a pile of ore, 7× production or 77× clicks for a while, or (rarely) a Diamond Comet with 777× production |
| **Warp Jump** | Prestige: after a billion ore in a run, start over in a new galaxy for Dark Matter. Every Dark Matter earned is +2.5% production (3.5% with Dark Matter Reactor) until the next Big Crunch, and it buys upgrades that last until then too |
| **Big Crunch** | The prestige after Warp Jump: after earning 200 Dark Matter in a universe, collapse it (Dark Matter, its upgrades and all) for Antimatter, √(Dark Matter ÷ 10). Every Antimatter earned is +10% production and +10% Dark Matter from Warp Jumps for good, and it buys permanent upgrades (keep the Dark Matter upgrades, twice the Dark Matter, start runs with ore, keep upgrades through Warp Jumps, stronger comets) |
| **Views** | A menu switches between three views without loading the page again (so the game keeps running): Ore (the asteroid, which stays put, next to the store, which scrolls), Dark Matter (the Warp Jump) and Antimatter (the Big Crunch, locked until the first Warp Jump). The menu shows your Dark Matter and Antimatter and a dot when there's something to do in a view. Stats, achievements and how to play are popups from the buttons by the title |
| **Extras** | 44 achievements, an asteroid that changes as you mine more, rock bits and floating numbers on every click, a news ticker, sounds and a leaderboard |
| **Hot keys** | Space mines, C catches a comet (after buying the Comet Hot Key upgrade), 1-9 and 0 buy the buildings in the order they're listed, U buys the cheapest affordable upgrade, A switches between buying 1/10/100, W opens the Warp Jump, B the Big Crunch and M turns the sound on/off. Holding Space only mines once |
| **Saving** | Saved on the server every 30 seconds and when you leave, and you keep mining at 5% while you're away. The server plays every click and purchase again to check it, so the leaderboard can't be cheated from the browser, and clicks only count up to 20 a second. Clicks at exactly the same pace for 20 seconds (an auto-clicker or a held-down key), or 40 clicks in a row whose gaps vary less than 10% (standard deviation of the gaps, as a share of the average gap), only mine 20%, until the pace changes. The game runs in one tab at a time |

The Games section can be opened to everyone, only to admins, or to nobody with `games_access` in the
[config](../config.md), and players can block it for themselves (see [Account & security](account.md)).
