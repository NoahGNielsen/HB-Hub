# 🛠️ For site admins

| | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The admin panel's start page (`/admin/`): a dashboard of the whole site, worked out each time it's opened, laid out as a grid of panels. A "Needs a look" strip at the top (new feedback, browser errors, crash reports, locked, ghost banned and banned accounts, each linking to its list; it says "All clear" when there's nothing new); four headline numbers (online now, active today, messages today with the change on the day before, and accounts with the change in new ones on the week before); bar charts of messages and new accounts each day for the last 30 days (with a tooltip per day and a table view); chat numbers, and the busiest chats and people of the week with bars comparing them; a bar chart of page views each day for the last 30 days and the 10 most visited pages of the week (with how many of their views weren't logged in), from a count per page and day that `header.php` keeps (`assets/php/pageViews.php`: never who, no bots, prefetches or error pages, kept 90 days); the chips in wallets and each casino game's rounds, players, chips bet and house result for the week, with a total; and each game's games and players for the week and games ever |
| **Admin panel** | Search every user or chat by name or id (or right-click one in the chat), and sort the lists by any column (status, joined, last seen; type, members, last activity, status). Users: info, logins, messages and attachments, and ban, edit (name, bio, status), remove their picture or banner picture, reset or delete them, delete their attachments or give or take away attachment tickets. Chats: members, pinned messages and messages, and rename, remove the icon, change the background, kick members, unpin messages or delete it |
| **Ghost bans** | Ghost ban someone from their admin panel page: they aren't told and the chat works like normal for them, but their messages, files, edits, reactions and the chats they start or add people to only show to themselves. Nobody gets a notification or request from them. Admins still see it all, marked as only seen by the sender. An account at most 3 days old that's made or logged in to in a browser the ghost-banned account uses is ghost banned too, by itself (it shows as "Automatic" in the admin log); older accounts aren't touched |
| **People** | Accounts that have been used in the same browser count as one person: known by the device cookie, or by a browser fingerprint (a hash of the canvas, graphics card, audio, fonts, screen, time zone and so on, sent about once a day while logged in) that survives clearing cookies and private windows (never for a browser that sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track: only its device cookie links it). A fingerprint shared by more than 4 accounts (identical school computers) doesn't link anyone. Under People in the admin panel's side menu (or the Person button on a user's page): every account side by side with its status, chips and messages, the devices and fingerprints that link them, all their sessions, and the gambling block, admin block, log out, ghost ban, ban and delete for any of their accounts at once (tick off any linked only by a shared computer). The Danger zone on a user's page also has a switch, off by default, to do the same to their other accounts |
| **Banned pictures** | Banning someone can ban their profile picture and banner picture too, and "Remove and ban" bans just the picture or banner: any account that uploads the same picture (or a resized or slightly cropped copy) as its profile picture or banner, or signs up with it, is banned straight away. Add, view and remove pictures under Banned pictures in the admin panel's side menu |
| **Feedback inbox** | Read what users send with the Feedback button, with who sent it and from which page (marked Dev when it came from the dev site), under Feedback in the admin panel's side menu. Mark it as done or delete it; the number of new ones shows on Admin in the side bar |
| **Announcements** | Send a notification to every user at once (everyone but banned and deleted accounts) under Announcements in the admin panel's side menu: a message of up to 500 characters and, if wanted, a page on the site it links to. It says "Announcement from the admins", not which admin. The list of sent ones shows who sent each, when, and how many have seen it, and Take back removes one from everyone's bell |
| **Crash reports** | The Ore Mining Inc. crash reports players agreed to send, under Crash reports in the admin panel's side menu: who, when, which browser, what the game was doing when it froze and for how long, the last things it noted down, its memory use, and the whole report with the save. Delete them, or they go by themselves after 90 days. Under them are the newest notes browsers sent by themselves when the tab ran out of memory or stopped responding (reason, time and browser, not who) |
| **Browser errors** | Every page notes the errors that happen in the browser (the Watchdog, `assets/js/watchdog.js`): answers of 400 or more from fetch(), XMLHttpRequest and, in Chromium, also pictures, styles and scripts (508 "Resource Limit Is Reached" is the server being busy); requests that got no answer; files that didn't load; uncaught JavaScript errors and promise rejections; and what the CSP blocked. Browser extensions' errors, aborted requests and ones broken off by leaving the page or being offline are left out. It reports them 3 seconds after the first one, trying again every 3 seconds until the report gets through (kept in sessionStorage meanwhile, so a reload doesn't lose it). Under Browser errors in the admin panel's side menu (with how many errors in the last 24 hours): totals and 508s for the last hour, day, week and 30 days, how many of each kind and the most common errors in the last 7 days, and every report with when, how many, who (or not logged in), the page they were on, each error (kind or HTTP status, address and line, message, how often), IP address, user agent and a Dev mark. Clear the reports takes them off the list (the numbers at the top still count them); they go for good by themselves after 30 days |
| **Admin log** | Everything admins do in the admin panel is logged, opening a user or chat included (as that shows their messages and personal info): who did what to whom, when, and why (every action asks for a reason in a popup first). Automatic bans for a banned profile picture or banner show up there too. Under Admin log in the admin panel's side menu, or the Admin log button on a user or chat. Can't be changed or deleted, kept for a year |
| **Maintenance mode** | Turn off logins and show a message, straight from the [site config](../config.md) |
| **Section switches** | Open the Casino and the Games section to everyone, only to admins, or to nobody |
